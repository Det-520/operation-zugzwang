## 1. Bottom line up front

- **[CANON]** **REDFOR = Cosmopolis**, a distant, rich, powerful, technologically advanced nation that calls itself a democracy with an apolitical military (the Cosmopolis National Defense Forces, **CNDF**). It is about **8,000 miles northeast** of the conflict.
- **[CANON]** **BLUEFOR = Omak.** An election scandal (revealed Cosmopolis backing of the Sapphire Party president) and wider discontent triggered an uprising. The apolitical **National Democratic Forces (NDF)** stood aside and let the **People's Freedom Alliance (PFA)**, the armed wing of the **Indigo Party**, take power.
- **[CANON]** The **Strait of Great Fortune**, a major trade chokepoint, is bounded by **Omak to the south** and **Tekoa to the north**.
- **[CANON]** Cosmopolis had trade deals and a defense treaty with the ousted Sapphire president, who **fled to Cosmopolis**.
- **[CANON]** **Asotin**, a peer adversary of Cosmopolis, **backs the PFA government in Omak**.
- **[CANON] Balance.** REDFOR starts with **more assets**. BLUEFOR has **natural advantages**: it is defending its own land, it has a **supportive population**, and it can **receive support from Asotin**.
- **[CANON] All assets are American.** Every asset in the game is a real U.S. military system, mostly **Department of the Air Force**, so cadets learn American capabilities. This applies to every actor, including Omak and Asotin. In-world, Omak's NDF bought its U.S.-type equipment from Cosmopolis under the old treaty. For Asotin it is simply a game convention.
- **[CANON]** The war begins with the white cell's kickoff inject at the end of Phase I.

### 3.1 Map [CANON]

The game map is **`map/maps/strait_map_v10_full.pdf`** (printable in pieces as `strait_map_v10_tiles_letter.pdf` in the same folder). `map/README.md` describes it and how to regenerate it. It replaces the designer's earlier v9 map, which put Camas out of PrSM range. The **Strait of Great Fortune** runs between the Tekoa Coast (north) and the Omak Coast (south), and is **25–75 miles wide** **[CANON]**. Latah and Camas Island are drawn red (pro-Cosmopolis), Tekoa green (neutral), and Omak blue. Asotin (~1,200 mi W) and Cosmopolis (~8,000 mi NE) are off the map.

It also shows bypass routes, a 100-mile grid, and spot heights. The distances in §3.3 are measured from it.

## 6. Timing and movement [CANON: ~3 days per turn; table PROPOSED]

"N" is the turn an RFF is submitted (Phase I RFFs count as Turn 1). A force can be used in the COA of the turn it arrives: an N+1 RFF submitted on Turn 3 arrives on Turn 4 and can be part of the Turn 4 COA.

| Movement | Takes |
| --- | --- |
| Any aircraft within theater (Camas ↔ Strait, Latah ↔ Strait) | Same turn (part of the COA) |
| Fighters, tankers, and ISR self-deploying from Cosmopolis | Arrive N+1 |
| Bombers striking from Cosmopolis | Same turn, but the bomber counter is used up for that turn |
| Airlifted light ground forces | N+2 |
| Naval forces from regional waters | N+2 |
| Amphibious forces | N+3 |
| Heavy ground forces by sea from Cosmopolis | **Beyond the game** (≥ 5 turns) |

| BLUEFOR mobilization | N+1 |

| Diplomatic results (basing agreements, recognition) | Same turn if the COA succeeds. Effects begin next turn. |

## 7. Rules of engagement and national directives (start of game) [PROPOSED]

These are public in the game world. The white cell may add, change, or lift directives with injects (`RULES.md` §7). That is the CANON tool for bounding behavior.

### 7.1 Cosmopolis (REDFOR)

1. No nuclear, chemical, or biological weapons (applies game-wide to all actors).
2. **No strikes on leadership or government targets in Omak City (Omak's capital)** without national authority (an RFF for authorities).
3. **No ground forces on Omak soil** without national approval.
4. **Respect Tekoan sovereignty**: no operations in Tekoan territory or airspace without Tekoa's consent.
5. **Do not engage Asotin forces or Asotin-flagged shipments** without national authority. Shipments that have already been delivered inside Omak are fair game.
6. Minimize civilian harm. A COA result causing significant civilian casualties moves International Opinion −1 **and** Domestic Will −1.

### 7.2 Omak (BLUEFOR)

1. The NDF chief's standing order: **the NDF will not fire on Omak civilians or take part in political repression.** Internal security is PFA-only.
2. Striking *civilian* targets on Camas or in Latah moves International Opinion **+1 (toward Cosmopolis)**, which hurts Omak. Striking **Latah** at all brings Latah into the war.
3. Attacking neutral shipping in the Strait (not Cosmopolis-flagged or military) moves International Opinion +1 (toward Cosmopolis).
