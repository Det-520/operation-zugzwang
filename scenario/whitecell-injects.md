## 1. Kickoff inject (end of Phase I) [CANON: the war begins; wording PROPOSED]

> "FLASH. Talks have collapsed. This morning the PFA government declared the Strait of Great Fortune **closed to Cosmopolis-flagged and allied military shipping**, and PFA maritime militia boarded and seized the Cosmopolis-flagged container ship **MV *Fortune's Promise*** and its crew of 22. Asotin has called the seizure 'a lawful act of a sovereign nation.' The President of Cosmopolis, invoking the defense treaty with Omak's 'legitimate' Sapphire government, has ordered military operations. Omak's government has declared that any attack will be met with full force. **A state of war exists.** Strait Traffic is now **Restricted**."

The seized ship and crew make a ready-made objective and bargaining chip for either side.

## 3. Tekoa (white-cell actor) [PROPOSED]

Tekoa is CANON neutral and sits on the north shore of the Strait, so both sides have real reasons to court it. The white cell plays it. Teams deal with it through COAs, like Latah or the press (`RULES.md` §3.5).

- **Has:** control of the north shore and Tekoan airspace (overflight), mountain sites for radar or SOF, local guides, tacit control of some pirate bands.
- **Can offer either side:** overflight or basing, radar sites, intel on pirate and smuggler routes, a "blind eye" to an Asotin overland supply route (or a block on it), or a crackdown on pirates.
- **Wants:** aid or money from both sides, no combat in Tekoan zones Z4 and Z5, and a seat at any Strait settlement with a share of Strait revenue.
- **Hidden agenda:** Tekoan clans profit from piracy, so Tekoa doesn't actually want the Strait fully secure.
- **Equipment, if ever needed:** a few **UH-1H** helicopters and **AN/TPS-75** radars (game convention: all assets are U.S. systems).
