### Things to think about

- No single COA will settle an objective. Plan a sequence.
- R1 and R2 both run through the tracks (Strait Traffic and Regime Stability). Decide which tracks you will push each turn.
- R3 requires stopping something Asotin hasn't done yet. Watch for it, and think about what authority you would need to act (R-39).
- A negotiated outcome can count. Talks are a weapon too.

## 2. Intelligence estimate

*This is the Joint Staff's assessment of what Cosmopolis believes. It may be incomplete or wrong. The Intelligence Products are the raw reporting behind it, and they don't all agree. Use RFIs and ISR COAs to confirm. All visible BLUEFOR counters are on the central map.*

- **Air defenses:** two Patriot battalions, PAT-1 (coast, covering the Narrows) and PAT-2 (Omak City, the capital), locations known. Short-range air defense systems are likely, but their number and locations are unknown.
- **Coastal anti-ship missiles:** at least one Harpoon coastal-defense battery (CB-1) near the western Narrows.
- **Long-range strike:** we never sold Omak PrSM missiles. Reports of HIMARS units in Omak are unconfirmed.
- **Mines:** Omak may have some aircraft-delivered mines.
- **Latah:** supports us. Basing talks are ongoing.
- **Asotin:** is arming Omak, extent unknown. Asotin is expected to avoid fighting us directly.
- **Sapphire loyalists:** there is significant loyalist sentiment in the NDF officer corps.

**Good first RFIs** might target the gaps above: where the short-range air defenses are, whether there are more anti-ship batteries, what Asotin has shipped, and how Latah's leaders are leaning.

## 4. Phase I & Phase III Planning

### Planning questions

1. Which objective do we push first, and which tracks does that move?
2. How do we use the 3 turns of high tempo before S-R1 bites, and when do we request sustainment?
3. How do we protect the Camas hub, or get off it?
4. Which intelligence products do we believe, which do we doubt, and which do we need to confirm before we act on them?
5. What are our **non-kinetic** COAs? Some ideas to test: a diplomatic push for Latah basing (S-R3), an information campaign to repair our legitimacy (S-R4), outreach to Tekoa for overflight or radar sites, pressure on Asotin's supply line by diplomacy rather than force, or an offer of talks that puts Omak's government on the table (R2).
6. What would we need national authority for (R-39), and when do we ask?
7. Which hidden asset do we reveal first, and why?
