**Summary.** NDF crews fitted steel rails to the rear ramps of two civilian car ferries and stacked heavy cylinders under guard on the quay beside them.

**Text.**

1\. Source states that NDF crews spent two nights stacking **heavy olive-green cylinders**, "longer than a man," on the quay at Fortune Harbor. The stack was kept under guard and covered with tarps.

2\. Workers were told the cylinders were "fuel bladders."

3\. Source also reports that NDF engineers fitted steel rails to the rear vehicle ramps of **two civilian car ferries** berthed beside the stack. The ferries remain in civilian service, and their crews were told the work was "maintenance."
