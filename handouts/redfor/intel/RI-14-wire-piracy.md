## Trawler Robbed off Tekoa Coast, Third Attack This Month

A fishing trawler was boarded and robbed off the Tekoa Coast overnight, the third such attack this month.

A spokesman for Tekoa's council of clans denied any involvement and said the council "cannot police every cove."

Local traders say several clans **collect a "harbor fee"** from the boats that operate out of their coves.
