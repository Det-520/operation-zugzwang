**THE COSMOPOLIS LEDGER** · D−6

## Poll: Public Split on Omak Action as Legislature Demands a Vote

By the *Ledger* Politics Desk

CAPITAL CITY — Cosmopolitans are divided over military action in the Strait of Great Fortune, according to a new survey released this morning.

**Forty-one percent** of respondents support military action to keep the Strait open. **Forty-eight percent** oppose it, and 11 percent are undecided. Support drops to **29 percent** "if Cosmopolis troops are killed."

Opposition leaders in the legislature are demanding a vote before any strikes. "The meddling scandal is the reason we are in this mess," one senior opposition member said. "The government doesn't get to start a war to cover it up."

The crisis is already reaching household budgets. Fuel prices rose 6 percent this week on fears that the Strait could close.
