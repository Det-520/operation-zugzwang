// COSMOPOLIS SECRET //

MEMORANDUM FOR THE MINISTER OF DEFENSE

FROM: Office of International Security Affairs

SUBJECT: Memorandum of Conversation: Call with the Latah Defense Minister

1\. Date and place. D−5, by secure telephone.

2\. Participants. The Cosmopolis Deputy Minister of Defense and the Latah Defense Minister, with note-takers.

3\. Summary. The minister reaffirmed Latah's friendship with Cosmopolis and its offer of **logistics access**. He would not commit to combat basing.

4\. Combat basing. Asked about combat basing, the minister said: "We cannot let combat aircraft fly from our soil **until our own skies are safe.** Omak's missiles can reach us, and Asotin is watching."

5\. Air defense. The minister asked twice what **air and missile defense** Cosmopolis could station in Latah.
