# RI-06 · Maritime Security Advisory

**REDFOR EYES ONLY.** *Fictional training scenario.*

---

**STRAIT MARITIME SECURITY CENTRE**\
**MARITIME SECURITY ADVISORY No. 14**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Issued** | D−4 |
| **Area** | Strait of Great Fortune, especially the Narrows |
| **Threat** | Harassment and attempted inspection of merchant vessels by armed small craft |

**1. Situation.** War-risk insurance premiums for Strait transits have risen **about 400%** in ten days. Two container lines have suspended Strait calls and are rerouting, which adds days to each voyage.

**2. Reported incidents.** Masters of **Cosmopolis-flagged vessels** report being **shadowed by small armed boats** in the Narrows. In two cases, the boats **hailed the vessel, identified themselves as PFA militia, and demanded to inspect the cargo**. Both vessels refused and continued.

**3. Guidance to masters.**

a. Transit in daylight.

b. Keep AIS switched on.

c. Report every approach by small craft to the Centre.
