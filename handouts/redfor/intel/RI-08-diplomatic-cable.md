# RI-08 · Diplomatic Cable

**REDFOR EYES ONLY.** *Fictional training scenario.*

---

**Rating:** B3

```text
COSMOPOLIS SECRET

FM: COSEMBASSY [NEUTRAL CAPITAL]
TO: MINFORAFF CAPITAL CITY
INFO: CNDF JOINT STAFF
DATE: D-10

SUBJECT: OMAK SEEKS RECOGNITION; HOSTS WATCHING OUR CONDUCT

1. SUMMARY: ENVOYS OF OMAK'S NEW GOVERNMENT HAVE VISITED AT
LEAST TWO NEUTRAL CAPITALS TO SEEK FORMAL DIPLOMATIC RECOGNITION.
ONE GOVERNMENT IS "SYMPATHETIC BUT CAUTIOUS" AND WILL DECIDE
BASED ON HOW COSMOPOLIS BEHAVES IN THE COMING WEEKS. END SUMMARY.

2. FOREIGN MINISTRY CONTACTS HERE CONFIRMED THAT OMAK ENVOYS
REQUESTED FORMAL RECOGNITION. A SENIOR OFFICIAL SAID HIS
GOVERNMENT WILL "WATCH HOW COSMOPOLIS BEHAVES IN THE COMING
WEEKS" BEFORE DECIDING.

3. IN THEIR MEETINGS THE ENVOYS HAVE STRESSED COSMOPOLIS'S
SUPPORT TO THE SAPPHIRE CAMPAIGN. HOST OFFICIALS RAISED IT
WITH US UNPROMPTED.

4. COMMENT: EACH REPORT OF CIVILIAN CASUALTIES WILL MAKE
RECOGNITION MORE LIKELY. RESTRAINT IS OUR BEST ARGUMENT
HERE. END COMMENT.

[AMBASSADOR]
```
