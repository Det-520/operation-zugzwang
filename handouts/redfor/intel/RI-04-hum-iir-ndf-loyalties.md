**Text.**

1\. Source states that **up to 60% of NDF field-grade officers** privately oppose the PFA and "will not fight for the militia's revolution."

2\. Source claims that a colonel in the NDF air defense command is "ready to hand over" Patriot positions and codes once Cosmopolis begins operations. Source did not name the colonel.

3\. Source states that NDF units in Omak City will stand aside again "just as they did three months ago."

**Comments.**

1\. (Collector comment) Source has a strong interest in encouraging Cosmopolis intervention.

2\. (Collector comment) Source's claims about specific officers have not been independently confirmed.
