**REDFOR EYES ONLY.** **Never show this to BLUEFOR.** *Fictional training scenario. All military systems are real U.S. systems, described with unclassified, approximate capabilities.*

**Counter states:** Ready · Degraded (−1 when used) · Destroyed (removed).\
**Visible** counters start on the central map, and BLUEFOR can see them. **Hidden** counters stay on this sheet until you use them or BLUEFOR finds them. In the tables below, hidden assets are the **shaded** rows (bold here).\
**Core function** (AFDP 3-0): AS = Air Superiority, GPA = Global Precision Attack, RGM = Rapid Global Mobility, GISR = Global ISR, C2 = Command and Control. **Joint** = Army, Navy, or Marine Corps.

You have **more assets than BLUEFOR**. Your problems are distance, a single forward base, and politics (Team Packet §3).
