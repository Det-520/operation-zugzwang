**Audience:** every player. **Printing:** each team needs 1 OPORD/FRAGO format page, 7 COA worksheets, 7 Cons worksheets, 9 pages of request slips (3 to a page, 25 slips), and 2 secret COA slips. The white cell's Print Packet already holds this many for both teams, plus spares; its Reference Forms version holds one of each per team, to copy from. The forms are black and white, so print them in greyscale. Requests, COAs, Cons, and secret COAs are **posted in Slack** (Rules Handbook §2.11): use the worksheets to draft, the copy-and-paste templates in §6 to post, and the paper slips only if Slack goes down.

## 6. Slack Templates

Copy a template into Slack, fill in the blanks, and post it. COAs and Cons go in your **group channel** (public); requests and secret COAs go in your **team channel** (secret). Use asset IDs (R-01, B-19b). Brief the same thing aloud in your slot: only what is said aloud counts.

### 6.1 COA (group channel, by the end of your COA slot)

```text
COA | Turn __ | REDFOR/BLUEFOR | Planned COA: Y/N
Action (who, what, where, which assets):
In order to (measurable effect):
Pro 1:
Pro 2:
Pro 3:
Public statement (optional):
```

### 6.2 Cons (group channel, by the end of your Cons slot)

```text
CONS | Turn __ | REDFOR/BLUEFOR
Their COA (in a few words):
Con 1:
Con 2:
Con 3:
```

### 6.3 Request (team channel, one message per request, during Prepare)

```text
RFI/RFF | Turn __ | REDFOR/BLUEFOR
RFI: (one question, one sentence)
RFF: Asset (pool ID or free text) | Destination | Purpose
```

### 6.4 Secret COA (team channel, once per phase)

```text
SECRET COA | Turn __ | REDFOR/BLUEFOR | Phase II/IV
The trap or deception (what, where, which assets):
Precise trigger (If ... then ...):
Intended effect:
```
