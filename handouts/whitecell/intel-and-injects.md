## 2. Answering RFIs

Answer from what that side would plausibly know: incomplete, uncertain ("unknown"), or wrong if the enemy has succeeded at deception. **Never** reveal secret objectives or secret COAs. Write answers in one or two sentences and hand them back at the start of the next Prepare.

- **Hidden counters** are revealed by a successful ISR **COA**, not by an RFI. An RFI can give hints ("signals intelligence suggests additional air defense emitters in the interior") but not a location.
- **An RFI that asks to confirm an intelligence product** gets an answer consistent with the key (§3): confirm what is true (without giving locations of hidden counters), cast doubt on what is false, and say "no further information" about noise.
- **BLUEFOR RFIs about REDFOR inside Omak** (SOF, loyalist contacts) get **full answers** while S-B5 holds (local lookouts).
- **BLUEFOR RFIs about Camas and naval movements** get **fuller answers** once B-41 (Asotin intel) has arrived.
- **REDFOR RFIs about what Omak can't hide** (visible counters, radar emissions, ships in port) get good answers. REDFOR has RC-135, RQ-4, P-8, and satellites.
- **Questions about white-cell actors' intentions** (Latah, Tekoa, Asotin) get a hedged answer that matches §1.

## 3. Intelligence product key

Both sides' products were written so that each team has **true hints** about the enemy's hidden assets and secret objectives, **misleading reports** that reinforce its estimate's errors, and **noise**. Use this key when a team builds a Pro or Con on a product (Handbook §5.2) or files an RFI about one.

**Verdicts:** **True** (accurate) · **Partly true** (accurate facts, wrong conclusion) · **False** (wrong or planted) · **Noise** (true but not decisive).

## 4. Injects

**At most one inject per turn**, delivered in the resolve step. Use injects to (a) add fog and friction, (b) force teams to adapt their plans, or (c) bound behavior that is going off the rails. There are no event cards.

### 4.1 Kickoff inject (read aloud at 1403)

> "FLASH. Talks have collapsed. This morning the PFA government declared the Strait of Great Fortune **closed to Cosmopolis-flagged and allied military shipping**, and PFA maritime militia boarded and seized the Cosmopolis-flagged container ship **MV *Fortune's Promise*** and its crew of 22. Asotin has called the seizure 'a lawful act of a sovereign nation.' The President of Cosmopolis, invoking the defense treaty with Omak's 'legitimate' Sapphire government, has ordered military operations. Omak's government has declared that any attack will be met with full force. **A state of war exists.** Strait Traffic is now **Restricted**."

Then move Strait Traffic to **Restricted**. The seized ship and crew are a ready-made objective and bargaining chip for either side.

**Track triggers** (Handbook §6.2) produce their own injects automatically, e.g. Domestic Will 0 → ceasefire order, Regime Stability 0 → government collapse.

**Choosing an inject.** Ask what the table needs. If one side is cruising, add friction against it. If both sides are only fighting, offer an off-ramp (I-12). If someone is going off the rails, use I-13. If nothing is needed, deliver nothing.
