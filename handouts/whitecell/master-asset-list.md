**WHITE CELL ONLY.** *Fictional training scenario. All military systems are real U.S. systems, described with unclassified, approximate capabilities.*

**Hidden** assets (off the map until found, used, or disclosed by an inject) are the **bold, shaded** rows. All others are visible on the central map from the start.\
**Core function** (AFDP 3-0, REDFOR Air Force assets): AS, GPA, RGM, GISR, C2. **Joint** = Army, Navy, or Marine Corps.\
**Totals:** REDFOR 22 counters (15 in theater, 7 at home, 3 hidden) · BLUEFOR 21 assets (5 hidden) on 29 counters, because B-06, B-07, and B-19 have 3 parts each and B-12 and B-18 have 2 (Rules §4.5).
