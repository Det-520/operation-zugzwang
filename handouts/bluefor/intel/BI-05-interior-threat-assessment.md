MEMORANDUM FOR THE PRESIDENT

FROM: Minister of the Interior

SUBJECT: Threat of a Cosmopolis Airborne Landing

1\. Assessment. The ministry assesses that **an airborne landing on the Omak coast is likely within 72 hours**.

2\. Basis.

a. Port workers on Camas report **C-17 transports unloading around the clock**. One worker was told that "three thousand paratroopers" are coming.

b. Cosmopolis has refused to rule out ground action.

3\. Recommendation. Move the armored brigades to the beaches.

&nbsp;

Minister of the Interior

---

1st Ind, Chief of the National Democratic Forces

MEMORANDUM FOR THE PRESIDENT

The NDF has not seen paratroopers on Camas. The C-17 cargo photographed so far is pallets, not people. The NDF does not concur with the recommendation in paragraph 3 at this time.

&nbsp;

Chief of the National Democratic Forces
