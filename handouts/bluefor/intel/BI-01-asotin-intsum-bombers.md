**ASOTIN DEFENSE INTELLIGENCE**\
**INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY FOR THE GOVERNMENT OF OMAK**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Number** | 0611 |
| **Date** | D−6 |
| **Sources** | Asotin satellite imagery and signals intelligence |
| **Subject** | Increased Readiness at a Cosmopolis Heavy-Bomber Base |
| **Rating** | B2 |

**1. Summary.** Cosmopolis has raised the readiness of the wing that operates its **stealth bombers**. The base is far from the theater, and Cosmopolis has not publicly linked this wing to Omak.

**2. Details.**

a. **All leave has been cancelled** for the wing.

b. Several aircraft have been moved into alert hangars.

c. Tanker activity near the base has doubled.

**3. Assessment.** Cosmopolis may be preparing bombers that Omak's current estimate does not account for.
