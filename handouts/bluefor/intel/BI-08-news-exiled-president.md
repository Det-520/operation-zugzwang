## Exiled President: "Omak Will Vote Again, Freely"

CAPITAL CITY — Omak's ousted president told supporters here on Sunday that he would be "home in Fortune Harbor before the harvest." He called for **new, internationally supervised elections** in Omak.

Separately, a group of Cosmopolis legislators introduced the **Omak Democracy Restoration Act**. The bill would fund an election in Omak "once the coup government steps aside."
