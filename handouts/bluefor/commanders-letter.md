EXECUTIVE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT

12 November 2026

Chief of the National Democratic Forces\
Commander, People's Freedom Alliance

Dear General Inslee and Commander Ferguson

I have written to you jointly so that each of you knows the other has received the same direction. The Republic's objectives are enclosed. I expect your two headquarters to plan the defense of Omak together, starting today.

Cosmopolis still refuses to recognize this government. It shelters the former president and calls our revolution a coup, and it has concentrated combat aircraft on Camas, about 270 miles from our coast, in the name of "freedom of navigation." Its Foreign Ministry says passage through the Strait is not negotiable. I take that as notice that it intends to use force.

I know there is distrust between the NDF and the PFA. The NDF did not fight in the revolution, and the PFA has not forgotten it. That history cannot be allowed to affect the defense of the country. Disputes over command, fuel, or units come to me for decision. They are not to be settled in the field. Cosmopolis does not need to defeat both of you if it can set you against each other.

Cosmopolis has more aircraft than we do, and better ones, and it sold us much of what we fly. Its weaknesses are also clear. Its forces are 8,000 miles from home, its public has little appetite for this war, and the election scandal has damaged its standing abroad. We are defending our own territory, and the population is with us. Preserve your forces. Do not offer the enemy targets it can destroy cheaply, and make every action it takes against us expensive.

Asotin has promised arms and intelligence, and its ambassador has assured me that Asotin will provide "whatever is needed." I intend to hold them to that. Its deliveries come by sea through the West Approach and by air. Keep both routes open.

My security services advise that Cosmopolis may land troops on our coast within days, and that Latah has agreed to host Cosmopolis bombers. Plan for both.

The Strait is our income, our leverage, and a matter of national sovereignty, and we will not give it up. How we hold it also matters. Other governments are deciding whether to recognize us, and they must see Omak defending itself, not threatening its neighbors or neutral shipping. Finally, some officers in the NDF may still be loyal to the former government. Commanders at every level should know their people.

You may speak for this government. I authorize you to negotiate in my name with Cosmopolis, directly or through intermediaries, and with Latah, Tekoa, Asotin, and any other government, and to conclude agreements without referring them to me first.

Sincerely

&nbsp;

ANTHONY L. RAY\
President of the Republic of Omak
