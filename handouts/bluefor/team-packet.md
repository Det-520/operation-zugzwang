**Your BLUEFOR read-ahead** is four documents: the **Commander's Read-Ahead** (your orders), this **Team Packet**, the **BLUEFOR Order of Battle** (your forces, your RFF pools, and the enemy forces you can see), and the **BLUEFOR Intelligence Products**. You also have the common documents: the Player Rules Handbook, the Scenario Brief, the Scenario Schedule, and the Player Forms.

## 1. Secret objectives

Keep these from REDFOR. The white cell scores them after Turn 7.

| # | Objective | Achieved | Partial | Not achieved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **B1** | **"Survive!"** | The Indigo/PFA government holds Omak City (the capital) and Regime Stability ≥ 2 | The government survives but Stability ≤ 1, or it is forced into a power-sharing deal | The government has fallen |
| **B2** | **"Keep the Strait ours!"** | Omak controls its southern shore, no Cosmopolis ground forces are on Omak soil, **and** any Strait arrangement recognizes Omak's sovereignty | Cosmopolis holds only a limited coastal foothold, **or** the Strait reopens on Cosmopolis's terms without Omak's agreement | Cosmopolis controls Omak's Strait coast |
| **B3** | **"Win the world's sympathy!"** *(long-term)* | International Opinion ≤ −2, **or** the government is formally recognized by at least two states besides Asotin | International Opinion −1 or 0 | International Opinion ≥ +1 |

### Things to think about

- No single COA will settle an objective. Plan a sequence.
- B1 runs through **Regime Stability**. Protect it from strikes, unrest, and NDF–PFA friction.
- B2 doesn't require the Strait to stay Closed. It requires that *Omak's sovereignty* is recognized in any arrangement. A deal can achieve it.
- B3 runs through **International Opinion**, which starts at −1. Every civilian you harm, every neutral ship you hit, and every lie you're caught in pushes it the wrong way.

## 2. Intelligence estimate

*This is your intelligence services' assessment of what Omak believes. It may be incomplete or wrong. The Intelligence Products are the raw reporting behind it, and they don't all agree. Use RFIs and ISR COAs to confirm. All visible REDFOR counters are on the central map.*

- **Cosmopolis bombers:** Cosmopolis can strike from home with B-52s and B-1s.
- **Cosmopolis special forces and cyber:** unknown.
- **Ground invasion:** Cosmopolis is likely planning a ground invasion within days.
- **Latah:** is Cosmopolis's ally and will host its bombers.
- **Asotin:** has promised arms, intel, and "whatever is needed."
- **Sapphire loyalists:** a few disloyal officers may remain in the NDF.

**Good first RFIs** might test the claims above: whether Cosmopolis ground forces or amphibious ships are actually moving, what Latah has really agreed to, and what Asotin will actually send. Remember that your RFIs about REDFOR activity *inside Omak* get full answers (S-B5).

## 4. Phase I & Phase III Planning

### Planning questions

1. Which objective is most at risk on Turn 1, and how do we protect it?
2. How do we survive REDFOR's first strikes? What do we disperse, hide, or move?
3. When, if ever, do we reveal our long-range strike option, and against what?
4. How do we fix the NDF–PFA seam (S-B1), and is it worth a COA?
5. Which intelligence products do we believe, which do we doubt, and which do we need to confirm before we act on them?
6. What are our **non-kinetic** COAs? Some ideas to test: an information campaign built on the meddling scandal (B3), a diplomatic push for recognition by other states (B3), counterintelligence against Sapphire loyalists (S-B2), an offer of talks that recognizes Omak's sovereignty over the Strait (B2), or outreach to Tekoa.
7. What do we ask Asotin for, and when? What would push Asotin to send more?
