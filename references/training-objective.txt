1.3 Training Objective: Apply wargaming principles to analyze and solve a tactical problem. 

 

Requirements: This training requires a dedicated classroom or briefing room with sufficient table space to accommodate the wargame for all participants. Cadets must be provided with pre-prepared scenario packets at least 24 hours in advance, detailing the operational environment, strategic context, intelligence reports, force laydowns, and clear objectives for all player factions. A specific wargaming system (e.g., board game, matrix game, or digital simulation) will be designated for the event. A "White Cell," composed of cadre or senior cadets, must be established to adjudicate game turns, introduce dynamic "fog of war" events, and ensure the scenario progresses according to the training objectives. All participants will be assigned specific roles (e.g., Commander, Ops, Intel) and must have access to relevant reference materials, such as excerpts from key joint and DAF doctrine. 

 

Performance Measures: 

    Develop a viable course of action (COA) to achieve the stated mission objectives. 

    Construct a clear and concise operations order (OPORD). 

    Execute the developed COA while justifying their decisions. 

    Modify the initial plan in response to adversary actions or White Cell injects. 

    Articulate key lessons learned during a post-game debrief. 

 

References: AFDP 3-0, Operations; AFDP 5-0, Planning; JP 3-0, Joint Campaigns and Operations; JP 5-0, Joint Planning; AFDP 1-1, Mission Command; AFDP 3-99, DAF Role in Joint All-Domain Operations (JADO) Related Lesson/s: Principles of Joint Operations and Tenants of Airpower (AS100); What the DAF Brings to the Fight (AS100); Problem Solving (AS200); Full Range Leadership (AS200); Critical Thinking 1 & 2 (AS200 & AS300); The Total Force (AS300); Managing Competing Priorities (AS300); Joint Operations (AS400); ACE (AS400), Military Planning Fundamentals (AS400); Capabilities and Force Packaging (AS400) 

 

Instructor: SCL with cadre guidance or Cadre 

 

Additional Information: This objective is designed to move cadets beyond theoretical knowledge of warfighting and into a dynamic environment where they must apply it. By engaging in a competitive wargame, cadets are forced to make decisions under pressure, manage limited resources, anticipate enemy actions, and adapt to unforeseen circumstances. This hands-on application develops the critical thinking, tactical acumen, and decisiveness required of warrior-minded leaders. It provides a direct, practical link between doctrinal concepts and their role in successfully employing warfighting assets, preparing them to lead in complex operational environments.
